# Slide-transition sound effects (worker / ffmpeg render)

Real sounds from **[@remotion/sfx](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/sfx/)** (remotion.media),
**MIT-licensed, normalized -3dB peak, no attribution required**. One per transition character,
selected by `_BROLL_SFX_MAP` in `compose.py` (mirror of `@sentezy/types` `BROLL_SFX_MAP`).

`whoosh.wav`, `whip.wav`, `page-turn.wav`, `switch.wav`, `shutter-modern.wav`.

Mirror of `apps/web/public/sfx/transitions/`. The older generic whooshes in `apps/worker/sfx/`
(`01-whoosh.mp3` …) are no longer used for transitions.
